# arXiv quant-ph submission readiness

Checked against official arXiv help on 2026-11-17. This is preparation only;
no submission has been made.

## Current rule and consequence

- arXiv may require endorsement before a user's first submission or a
  submission to a new category. The actual requirement is account- and
  category-dependent and is shown after a submission is started.
- An institutional email can expedite endorsement. Having no institutional
  email does **not** bar submission: the official alternative is personal
  endorsement by an established arXiv author.
- To request personal endorsement, start a submission, choose `quant-ph`, and
  use the request link sent by arXiv. Prefer an advisor, professor, or other
  active author who knows the submitter or the subject. Recent cited arXiv
  papers can be used to locate eligible endorsers through the “Which authors
  of this paper are endorsers?” link on an abstract page.
- At least one positive endorsement is required for the relevant endorsement
  category. Do not mass-email potential endorsers or repeatedly solicit the
  same person.
- arXiv does not publish a fixed paper-count threshold for `quant-ph` on the
  help page. Eligibility depends on the subject area; qualifying activity
  counts papers submitted from three months to five years ago, and the
  endorser must themselves have active positive endorsement. Do not claim a
  specific number such as four without the arXiv interface confirming it.
- Endorsement is a community/category check, not peer review or validation of
  the paper's correctness.

## Practical preparation without an institutional email

1. Create or update the arXiv account with a stable email and accurate identity;
   connect ORCID if available.
2. Prepare the final TeX source, figures, bibliography, and compiled PDF, then
   start a submission with primary category `quant-ph` (Quantum Physics).
3. If arXiv requests endorsement, save its request link/code. Send one concise
   request to a suitable eligible author with the title, abstract, draft PDF,
   ORCID or scholarly profile, and why the work fits `quant-ph`.
4. Submit only after the endorsement and the repository/Zenodo citation are
   settled. Category acceptance and moderation remain arXiv decisions.
